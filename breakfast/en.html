Woke up hungry?
We got you

Can't imagine a morning without a proper dish on your plate? Tex-Mex and Mexican-style breakfasts are waiting for you at Plac Wolnica in Kraków. Los Gorditos' burritos, bowls, tacos and quesadillas now in a fresh breakfast edition with fresh veggies and eggs. Try our breakfast drink specials too: espresso horchata, mimosa and bellini are the perfect match for your morning meal.

What's for breakfast?

Mornings here are all about flavor and energy. In Los Gorditos' new breakfast edition you'll find fresh takes on our classics. Add aromatic coffee or refreshing lemonade, and your day starts right.

Breakfast burrito

Flour tortilla filled with fresh veggies, fluffy eggs, and guacamole with tomatoes. Served with your salsa of choice and nachos.

Breakfast bowl

Everything great from a burrito, served in a bowl with feta and pickled red onion. No tortilla, but still delicious and filling. Served with your salsa of choice and nachos.

Breakfast tacos (3 pcs)

Three soft corn tortillas filled with fluffy eggs and fresh vegetables. Perfect for trying different proteins and sauces. Served with your salsa of choice and nachos.

Breakfast quesadilla

Hot, crispy flour tortilla filled with cheese, eggs, and your protein of choice. Served with selected salsa and sauce.

Horchata and coffee

If you cannot imagine breakfast without great coffee, we've got you covered. Sweet, refreshing, cinnamon espresso horchata is a perfect morning pick.

Jarritos

Add a splash of color to your morning. Mexican fruit sodas are already chilled and waiting in our fridge.
